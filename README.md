# Cash Book 📘

**Cash Book** is a powerful, secure, and privacy-focused personal finance manager for Android. Built with modern Android technologies, it helps users track their income, expenses, and savings with ease while providing deep insights through visual analytics.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Transaction Management**: Effortlessly record income and expenses with categories, notes, and timestamps.
- **Visual Analytics**: Dynamic "Pizza" (Pie) charts and monthly summaries to visualize spending patterns at a glance.
- **Multi-Account Support**: Manage multiple profiles for different users or purposes (e.g., Personal, Business).
- **Notes & Shopping Lists**: Built-in notepad for quick reminders and checkable shopping lists.
- **Privacy & Security**:
  - **App Lock**: Protect your sensitive financial data with a 4-digit PIN.
  - **Encrypted Backups**: Secure your data with AES-256 encryption before backing up to Google Drive.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: A professional navy-blue aesthetic tailored for both daytime and night-time use.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **UI**: Jetpack Compose (Modern declarative UI)
- **Database**: Room (Local persistent storage)
- **Architecture**: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) with StateFlow
- **Theme**: Material 3
- **Stability**: LeakCanary (Memory leak detection) & Firebase Crashlytics (Ready to enable)
- **Charts**: Custom Canvas-based interactive Pizza Charts

## 🔒 Security

We take your data privacy seriously. **Cash Book** uses industry-standard AES-256 encryption for backups. Even if your cloud storage is compromised, your financial data remains unreadable without your master backup password.
